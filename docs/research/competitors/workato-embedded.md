# Workato Embedded

## Observation (checked 2026-10-23)

- **What it is:** an enterprise automation platform with an Embedded add-on.
- **Positioning:** "Embedded Integrations at Scale" ([embedded](https://workato.com/ai/embedded))
- **Embedding options** ([OEM docs](https://docs.workato.com/en/oem.html)):
  - "Branded Access" (a white-label Workato UI)
  - a full embed by iframe or JWT
  - a connection widget for authorisation only
  - partner APIs, with dev, test and prod environments per customer
- **Agents and MCP:** not found on the embedded pages. They do exist on the main platform.
- **Integrations claimed:** "1200+ pre-built connectors" on the embedded page against "17,000+ apps, APIs, databases, files, MCP servers" on the homepage.
- **Pricing:** not public ([pricing](https://www.workato.com/pricing)).
- **Proof:**
  - "500+ SaaS companies trust Workato Embedded".
  - Logos: Ivanti, SmartRecruiters, Shutterstock.
  - Compliance badges were not visible on the pages fetched. The trust center did not load.

## Interpretation

- Workato appears in enterprise deals as the incumbent.
- Its sales-led, enterprise-only motion is the opposite of viaSocket's self-serve developer motion.

## Recommendation (Proposed)

- Monitor quarterly.
- **Watch:** https://workato.com/ai/embedded, https://www.workato.com/pricing, https://docs.workato.com/en/oem.html
