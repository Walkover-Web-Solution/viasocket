# viaSocket pricing

Machine-readable summary of workflow plans at [/pricing](/pricing). Amounts are in USD. Tasks and AI credits reset monthly; connected accounts, users, workflows and projects are unlimited on every plan.

## Free

- **Price:** $0 per month (annual billing: $0 per year)
- **Limits:** 100,000 tasks per month; 1,000 AI credits per month. Usage stops at either limit until the next month.
- **Includes:** 2,300+ integrations

## Pro (launch price)

- **Price:** $19 per month, or $190 per year (2 months free on annual billing)
- **Limits:** 250,000 tasks per month; 10,000 AI credits per month included
- **Extra usage:** $0.25 per 1,000 tasks; $1 per 600 AI credits
- **Includes:** 2,300+ integrations

## Business (launch price)

- **Price:** $99 per month, or $990 per year (2 months free on annual billing)
- **Limits:** 1,000,000 tasks per month; 50,000 AI credits per month included
- **Extra usage:** $0.15 per 1,000 tasks (40% cheaper than Pro); extra AI credits at 40% lower rates than Pro
- **Includes:** 2,300+ integrations

## Enterprise

- **Price:** Custom (billed annually)
- **Limits:** Custom tasks and AI credits per month
- **Extra usage:** Up to 60% cheaper than lower tiers for extra tasks and AI credits
- **Includes:** 2,300+ integrations; custom terms and support

## Units

- **Task:** One API call, action run, automation step, or one delivered trigger event (polls with no event are not billed).
- **AI credit:** Pays for AI, built-in tools and third-party data on steps that use them (1 task plus credits per such step).
